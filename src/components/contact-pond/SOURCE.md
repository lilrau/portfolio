# Koi pond source

The procedural koi renderer, anatomy and simulation in `renderer.ts`, `shape.ts` and `world.ts` are adapted from [Dao-Ho/portfolio](https://github.com/Dao-Ho/portfolio/tree/7fe372a1bd1308b8683e2e97bc6fe51522e2912f/src/app/components/koi-pond), revision `7fe372a1bd1308b8683e2e97bc6fe51522e2912f`.

Local adaptations: cool indigo and blue palette matching the portfolio, smaller population for a contact section, and a section-scoped React lifecycle with visibility, reduced-motion and interaction handling. No analytics integration.
